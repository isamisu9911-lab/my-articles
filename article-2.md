# How to Turn Your GitHub Repository into a Free Website

Did you know you can publish a free website directly from GitHub? It is called GitHub Pages, and it works great for blogs and articles.

## What is GitHub Pages?

GitHub Pages is a free hosting service from GitHub. It takes the files in your repository and shows them as a real website that anyone can visit.

## Steps to Turn It On

1. Open your repository and click **Settings**.
2. In the left menu, click **Pages**.
3. Under "Branch", choose **main** and click **Save**.
4. Wait a minute or two. Your site link will appear at the top of the page.

## Why Use It?

- It is completely free.
- You do not need to buy hosting.
- Every time you edit a file, your website updates automatically.

## Final Thoughts

If you already write articles on GitHub, turning on Pages is a small step that makes your work look like a real blog. Give it a try!
